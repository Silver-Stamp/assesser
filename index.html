● Call mode 00:00

Put the call on speaker. Tap "Me talking" when you speak and "Client talking" when they do. Both sides are used: your questions give meaning to the client's answers ("Are you a smoker?" → "No" means non-smoker). Options you list only count once the client picks or confirms one. Matches and follow-up questions update live.

● Computer call 00:00

For calls on a computer in Chrome or Edge. Take the call on the computer: a browser-tab phone (Google Voice, Quo), Phone Link on Windows, or iPhone calls on a Mac. Wear a headset. When asked what to share, pick the call's tab (turn on "Share tab audio") or your screen (turn on "Share system audio"). Your mic is labeled Me and the call audio is labeled Client automatically, so no tapping. The free speech model runs on this computer; the audio is never uploaded.

Dictation works in Chrome and Edge. Keep talking; the text goes into the notes box as you speak.
